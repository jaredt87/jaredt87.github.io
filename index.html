HOW I THINK ABOUT THE WORK

Good engineering is
more than making it work.

01

Make the safe path the easy path.

Build repeatable workflows that reduce manual steps and make the right action straightforward.

02

Prove what changed.

Keep observation, authorization, execution, and verification distinct. Treat evidence as part of the system, not an afterthought.

03

Optimize for the people operating it.

Reliable automation should reduce toil, clarify ownership, and give teams room to move independently.
